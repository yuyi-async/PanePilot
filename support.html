常见问题

首次使用需要设置哪些权限？

跟随首次启动的引导完成「自动化」授权（用于打开并排列项目文件）。「屏幕录制」为可选权限，仅用于窗口总览的缩略图显示，拒绝不影响基础分屏功能。

如何快速切换分屏组合？

按下 ⌥Tab 呼出组合切换面板，Tab 循环选择目标组合，Return 确认，窗口布局即恢复原样——两步完成切换。

组合是如何保存的？

确认一次分屏后，布局会自动保存为组合；你也可以在窗口总览中点选或框选窗口与项目候选，主动创建组合。所有数据仅保存在本地。

不想记快捷键，能用鼠标分屏吗？

可以。鼠标悬停窗口左上角绿色按钮，即可在九种布局中一键选择：半屏、四分、最大化等。

如何卸载并清理数据？

将 App 移入废纸篓即可；项目、分组与组合数据都存放在 App 沙盒容器内，会一并删除，无残留。

FAQ

What permissions do I need to set up?

Follow the first-launch guide to grant Automation access (used to open and arrange project files). Screen Recording is optional — it only enables window thumbnails in the overview; basic tiling works without it.

How do I switch combos quickly?

Press ⌥Tab to summon the switcher, Tab to cycle to the combo you want, then Return to confirm — the exact layout is restored in two steps.

How are combos saved?

Confirming a tiling automatically saves the layout as a combo. You can also create combos in the overview by clicking or marquee-selecting windows and project candidates. All data stays on your Mac.

Can I tile with the mouse only?

Yes. Hover a window's green button and pick from nine layouts — halves, quarters, fullscreen and more, in one click.

How do I uninstall and remove my data?

Move the app to the Trash. Projects, groups and combos live in the app's sandbox container and are removed with it — nothing is left behind.
