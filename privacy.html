生效日期：2026-11-21

PanePilot（下称「本 App」）是一款 macOS 窗口分屏与管理工具。我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明本 App 对数据的处理方式。

我们不收集任何数据

本 App 不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人数据：

  • 不内置任何分析、统计、广告或追踪 SDK；
  • 不联网上传你的任何操作、文件或窗口信息；
  • 不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱或设备标识。

数据全部保存在你的设备本地

你的项目文件夹、分组、分屏组合（窗口布局）、偏好设置等全部保存在本 App 的 macOS 沙盒容器中，仅由本 App 在本地读写，不会发送到任何服务器。卸载 App 即随之删除。

权限用途说明

本 App 申请的系统权限仅用于实现功能本身，均不构成数据收集：

  • 自动化（Apple Events）：用于以你选择的方式打开项目文件并排列窗口；
  • 屏幕录制（可选）：仅用于在窗口总览中显示窗口缩略图；拒绝授权不影响基础分屏功能。

未成年人

本 App 不收集任何数据，适合所有年龄段使用。

PanePilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21

PanePilot ("this app") is a macOS window tiling and management tool. We take your privacy seriously; this policy explains how the app handles data.

We do not collect any data

This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data:

  • It contains no analytics, statistics, advertising, or tracking SDKs;
  • It never uploads your actions, files, or window information to the internet;
  • It requires no account and collects no name, email, or device identifier.

All data stays on your device

Your project folders, groups, tiling combos (window layouts), and preferences are stored entirely inside the app's macOS sandbox container. They are read and written locally by this app only and are never sent to any server. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions and their purposes

The system permissions this app requests are used solely to provide its features and do not constitute data collection:

  • Automation (Apple Events): to open your project files with your chosen apps and arrange windows;
  • Screen Recording (optional): used only to show window thumbnails in the overview. Declining it does not affect basic tiling features.

Children

This app collects no data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, updates will be published on this page alongside app releases.

If you have questions, please reach us via the contact shown on the Support page.
